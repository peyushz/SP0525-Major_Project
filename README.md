# SP0525-Major_Project
Reflectra – Productivity & Time Intelligence App

It is designed for people who often feel:

“Where did my whole day go?”

“Why am I busy but not productive?”

“Why do I feel tired even after finishing tasks?”

Reflectra helps users understand how they actually spend their time, recognize patterns in their behavior, detect early signs of burnout, and build sustainable work habits — through a clean, intuitive digital experience.

The Problem

In today’s fast-paced digital world, most productivity apps focus only on tasks.

They:

Overload users with complex features

Focus only on to-do lists

Ignore emotional and mental patterns

Don’t show long-term behavior trends

Fail to detect burnout early

As a result, users:

Track tasks but don’t understand time usage

Complete work but feel exhausted

Start strong but lose consistency

React to deadlines instead of planning consciously

The core issue is not lack of tools.
It is lack of clarity and self-awareness.

The Solution – Clarity Through Reflection

Reflectra shifts productivity from task execution to time awareness.

Instead of overwhelming users, Reflectra simplifies time management by:

Tracking daily activities

Analyzing behavioral patterns

Visualizing productivity trends

Detecting burnout signals

Encouraging consistency through rewards

Providing clear, actionable insights

The goal is simple:

Move users from reactive task completion
to
conscious time planning and sustainable productivity.

Project Vision

The core vision behind Reflectra is:

Clarity through reflection.

Reflectra doesn’t just ask:
“What did you complete today?”

It asks:
“How did you spend your time?”
“How did you feel while doing it?”
“Are you moving toward your goals?”
“Are you overworking without realizing it?”

It turns time into visible data — and data into self-awareness.

User Flow Overview

Reflectra follows a structured and intuitive journey:

1️.Onboarding & First Interaction

Splash Screen

Welcome Screens

Sign Up / Authentication

Initial Setup (mood preference & time goals)

This stage introduces users to the idea of mindful time tracking and prepares them to start immediately without friction.

The experience is welcoming, not overwhelming.

2️.Dashboard – The Control Center

The Dashboard acts as the heart of the app.

It provides:

Daily progress overview

Time spent vs. goal comparison

Quick Smart Insight preview

Add Activity shortcut

Focus Session entry

The layout uses strong visual hierarchy, minimal distractions, and clear call-to-action elements to keep users focused.

3️.Activity Management Flow

Add Activity screen

Category selection

Time duration input

Mood tagging

Each activity is not just logged — it becomes part of a larger behavioral pattern.

Once added, activities automatically sync with the Timeline and Analytics modules.

4️.Timeline System

Timeline visually represents daily time distribution.

Features:

Structured time blocks

Color-coded categories

Clear activity segmentation

Seamless dashboard integration

It gives users instant visual awareness:
“Is my time aligned with my priorities?”

5️.Focus Session

The Focus Session helps users enter deep work mode.

Timer-based session

Minimal UI

Progress indicator

Completion feedback

It reduces digital distractions and builds discipline through consistent sessions.

6️.Analytics & Smart Insights

This is where Reflectra becomes intelligent.

Instead of raw data, users see meaningful insights:

 Mood vs Productivity comparison
Burnout Risk Detection
Time Goal Performance analysis
Personalized suggestions

This helps users make data-driven improvements rather than guessing where time is lost.

7️.Reward System

To support long-term engagement:

Streak tracking

Achievement badges

Positive reinforcement

The gamification is subtle — motivating without overwhelming.
